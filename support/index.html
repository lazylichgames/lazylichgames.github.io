Common questions

Where is my progress saved?
On your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

An ad did not give me the reward.
Rewards are given only when an ad is watched to the end. If you did and got nothing, email us with the time it happened.

Can I play offline?
Yes. Your undead keep working while you are away, and you collect it when you come back. Ads need a connection.
